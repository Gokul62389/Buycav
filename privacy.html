2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • When you email, call or WhatsApp us: your name, phone number, email address and what you write to us.
  • When you use the contact form or store locator: the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email, town or district. To confirm: the exact fields on the live forms.

Information collected automatically

  • Essential cookies that make the site work and remember your cookie choice.

We do not collect payment details, government ID numbers or health information through this website.

  • CAV shops and dealers, only when you ask us to connect you with a shop, so they can help you.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose above, then delete it. As a guide:

  • Messages and enquiries: up to 12 months after our last reply.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months.
  • Your cookie choice: 12 months, after which we ask again.

If you withdraw your consent, or the purpose is complete, we delete the data unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Ask what we hold about you, how we use it, and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete or update your data.
  • Ask us to delete your data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. For cookies, use .
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act.
  • Complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including the right to object and to data portability.

To use any right, email main@bosymcorp.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act.
